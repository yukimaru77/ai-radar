Codex Business: 5h後に週トークン323M→173M（約46%）

https://www.reddit.com/r/codex/comments/1w1613f/

r/codex の Palastruka（Arctic Shift ダンプ、コメント0・スコア1時点）。タイトル: 「Did OpenAI just quietly cut actual Codex usage nearly in half?」Business Standard 席で、5分刻みの自前ログが 5h 窓導入前後で週トークンをほぼ半減させた、という現場声。

I've been tracking my Codex usage every 5 minutes since July 29 — 14,744 snapshots so far.

I'm on a Business Standard seat, not Premium, and when I compared my data before and after the 5h window was introduced, I found this:

Before: ~323M tokens/week
Now: ~173M tokens/week
That's roughly 46% fewer effective tokens per week.

著者自身も「完全サイクルはまだ少なく、46% を確定とは言わない」と留保しつつ、「323M→173M は無視できない差」と続ける。Plus / Business Standard で前後ログを持つ人への呼びかけで閉じる。

I still only have a few complete cycles under the new system, so I'm not claiming the 46% is definitive yet. But going from ~323M to ~173M is way too big a difference to ignore.
