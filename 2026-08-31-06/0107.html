「Cogram Studio (studio.cogram.com) is a CAD and BIM workspace for AI agents to create three-dimensional models and dimensioned drawings.」FreeCAD 1.1 headless + OpenCASCADE。STEP / IFC / STL / DXF / FCStd。MCP で「you can operate it from Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, or other agents」。内蔵剤は Pi（earendil-works/pi）。

試し方は2つ。(1) BYO agent。「Point Codex or Claude Code at studio.cogram.com/skill.md and ask it to follow instructions there. This starts a one-hour Studio session」。(2) 内蔵剤。「Submit a prompt at studio.cogram.com and sign up. You receive 50 free credits。」保存したいときだけ sign up。

サンプル。本棚 studio.cogram.com/view/shr_1agb21nd4。Colosseum studio.cogram.com/view/shr_3tm32c64y（大きい、数分かかることがある）。現場の警告。「If you ask an agent for a finished, complex model and "let it rip", it may produce something that looks convincing but doesn't hold up to scrutiny.」「Agentic modelling feels roughly like where agentic programming was in 2024」。
