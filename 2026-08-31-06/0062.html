README: RL training environments for Microduck — a ~800 g, ~25 cm tall bipedal robot — built on mjlab (MuJoCo Warp) with PPO. Policies are trained here at 50 Hz, exported to ONNX, and deployed on the real robot by the runtime in pollen-robotics/microduck。

Quickstart は CUDA GPU と uv。ARM boxes (DGX Spark / GB10, Jetson): uv sync pulls ~2 GB of CUDA wheels on first run。train Mjlab-Velocity-Flat-MicroDuck --env.scene.num-envs 4096。~1-2 h for a usable gait at 4096 envs。export.py で ONNX。No GPU なら --hf-jobs。

Tasks 例: Velocity（walking + head-pose）、VelStand（fall+recover）、StandUp、SitStand、GroundPick、BallKick、Roulade、Rollers。sim2real recipe: BAM actuator physics, domain randomization, backlash simulation。
